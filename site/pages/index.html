Always know the hour you’re in.

AstroHour lives in your status bar and shows the current planetary hour, the phase of the Moon, the lunar day and the sign the Moon is travelling through. Glance at the icon, hover for the details, or open the popup for the whole day.

What it does

Everything the day is made of, at a glance

♃

Planetary hours

The day and night are each split into twelve hours, ruled by the seven classical planets. AstroHour works them out from the real sunrise and sunset where you are.

☽

Moon, lunar day & sign

Phase, illumination, lunar day and the Moon’s zodiac sign, in either the tropical (Western) or sidereal (Vedic, Lahiri) zodiac. You choose.

✦

A quiet tray icon

The tray shows just the planet’s symbol. Hover for the details. Colour it Auto, White, Gold or any colour you like.

♀

The whole day

See every hour of today, page to other days, and jot a note against any hour.

🔔

Gentle reminders

Optional hour-change notifications and heads-up reminders before an hour ends, with a choice of sounds. Everything is off until you turn it on.

☉

Private by design

Everything is calculated on your device. Your location never leaves it, and the app makes no network requests at all.

Build it on Linux

You need Rust, Node.js 18+ and the Tauri prerequisites for your distribution.

git clone {{github_url}}
cd astrohour
npm install
cargo install tauri-cli
cargo tauri build
