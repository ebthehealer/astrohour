Privacy

The short version: AstroHour collects nothing about you.

  • The app makes no network requests. It has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting.
  • Your location (the coordinates used for sunrise and sunset) is stored only on your device. If you use “Use my location”, the request is answered by your operating system and the result stays on your device.
  • Your notes and settings are stored in the app’s data folder on your computer. Uninstalling the app and deleting that folder removes them.
  • This website is static. It sets no cookies and runs no analytics scripts.
  • Donations are processed by Stripe. Stripe collects the payment details and email address you give it at checkout and handles them under its own privacy policy. AstroHour never sees your card details.

Donations & refunds

  • Donations are voluntary gifts to support development. They do not buy features, a licence or support priority. AstroHour is free whether or not you donate.
  • Donations are one-time payments and are not recurring.
  • Stripe emails you a receipt for each payment. Donations are not tax-deductible.
  • If you donated by mistake, or the wrong amount, write to {{email}} within 30 days and we’ll refund it.
